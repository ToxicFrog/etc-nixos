Listening in the Browser (Polaris)

Browse to music.ancilla.ca. Log in, find your music in the file tree or using the filter bar, and drag and drop it to the right side of the screen to build the play queue. You can reorder the queue by clicking and dragging, and remove things from the queue with the delete key.

The Genres/Artists/Albums tabs might work, but there is a lot of music in the collection that doesn't support those tags, and a smaller set where the tags are incorrect, so they may not work as well as one might hope.

If you have ended here by mistake and want to play music on the household speakers, click on House Speakers on the left to go to Snapcast instead.

Playing Music in the House (Snapcast)

This is only accessible from inside the home network.

Browse to snapcast.ancilla.ca/mpd. Click Music to browse the music collection or use the sidebar to access the now-playing queue.

If you want to adjust the volume of individual speakers or turn them on or off, click Speaker Volume to access the Snapcast control page. Speakers set to all or music will play music; speakers set to station will only play chimes and voice announcements.

To play music in the browser as well as via the speakers, open Speaker Volume and click the play button in the top right. If you just want to listen in the browser, click Listen in Browser to go to Polaris instead.
